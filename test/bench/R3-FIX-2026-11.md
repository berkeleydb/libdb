# R3 eviction-sweep fix: A/B benchmark (2026-11)

Bounding the COOL-first eviction sweep (review item O-MP1, regression R3),
branch `perf/evict-bound`, design note [`rfc/0002/R3-FIX.md`](../../rfc/0002/R3-FIX.md).

## Host and method

- **Host:** c6id.metal (128 vCPU, 2 sockets), Debian 12, local NVMe formatted XFS
  at `/data`, `noatime`. Tuned: `performance` governor, THP `never`,
  `swappiness=1`, `numa_balancing=0`.
- **Arms:** `base` = `origin/master` (e1fe5c2c4, the regression); `fix` =
  `perf/evict-bound`; `bdb` = Berkeley DB 5.3.28 (tag `v5.3.28`). All three built
  with `--enable-shared --enable-static --enable-o_direct` and the XREL CFLAGS
  (`-O2 -g -std=gnu99 -Wno-error=implicit-int …`).
- **Probe:** `xrel_evict_probe` (1M sequential inserts, `DB_PRIVATE` mpool-only
  env, no transactions), the same probe that produced the R3 table in
  `XREL-2026-10.md`. 5 reps, interleaved, arm order rotated each rep. Raw data in
  `results/r3-fix-2026-11/evict_ab.tsv` (CV ≤ 1.5% on every cell).
- **Region signature:** with identical configure flags, `base` and `fix` both
  print `__env_struct_sig() = 0xe67a9de8` — byte-identical, so the fix is
  **format-free** (no region/ABI change).

## Result: buckets examined per allocation (the regression metric)

`st_alloc_buckets / st_alloc` — the ratio `db_stat -m` reports. 5.3.28 sat at
1–2; master's COOL-first sweep ran the whole table.

| am | cache | base | **fix** | bdb 5.3.28 |
|----|------:|-----:|--------:|-----------:|
| btree | 1 MB | 130.4 | **17.4** | 2.0 |
| btree | 4 MB | 459.3 | **15.6** | 1.8 |
| btree | 16 MB | 275.5 | **9.4** | 1.1 |
| btree | 64 MB (fits) | 0.0 | **0.0** | 0.0 |
| queue | 1 MB | 133.3 | **16.7** | 1.9 |
| queue | 4 MB | 520.1 | **13.8** | 1.6 |
| queue | 16 MB | 79.8 | **2.7** | 0.3 |
| queue | 64 MB (fits) | 0.0 | **0.0** | 0.0 |

## Result: throughput (ops/s, median of 5)

| am | cache | base | **fix** | bdb | fix/base | fix/bdb |
|----|------:|-----:|--------:|----:|---------:|--------:|
| btree | 1 MB | 2,363,686 | 2,476,676 | 2,824,165 | **1.05×** | 0.88× |
| btree | 4 MB | 2,132,815 | 2,490,170 | 2,818,537 | **1.17×** | 0.88× |
| btree | 16 MB | 2,137,515 | 2,517,319 | 2,834,612 | **1.18×** | 0.89× |
| btree | 64 MB | 2,575,859 | 2,588,206 | 2,887,378 | **1.00×** | 0.90× |
| queue | 1 MB | 183,739 | 4,753,823 | 6,062,737 | **25.9×** | 0.78× |
| queue | 4 MB | 140,454 | 4,916,663 | 6,029,290 | **35.0×** | 0.82× |
| queue | 16 MB | 4,804,228 | 5,043,068 | 6,151,613 | **1.05×** | 0.82× |
| queue | 64 MB | 5,141,600 | 5,132,127 | 6,244,380 | **1.00×** | 0.82× |

- The catastrophic queue regression (XREL R3: 0.05× of 5.3.28 at 4 MB) is gone:
  the fix is **35×** master and now **0.82×** of 5.3.28.
- btree under cache pressure recovers 1.05–1.18×.
- **64 MB (data fits, no eviction): 1.00× vs master** — the fix does nothing when
  the eviction path is not exercised, as required.
- The residual 0.82–0.90× gap to 5.3.28 is the single-threaded put cost
  (detector R1 + cursor-sharding R2, fixed separately in v2026.10.8), not the
  eviction sweep — consistent with XREL's note that the in-cache 64 MB case was
  already 0.85× from that cost.

## Result: dirty evictions (the thrash the sweep caused)

`st_rw_evict` (pages forced from the cache), queue, median:

| cache | base | **fix** | bdb |
|------:|-----:|--------:|----:|
| 1 MB | 1,788,389 | **5,607** | 5,607 |
| 4 MB | 849,148 | **4,676** | 4,676 |

The unbounded sweep evicted ~180× more pages than needed; the bounded sweep
matches 5.3.28 exactly. Fewer, better-chosen victims is most of the throughput
win.

## Profile (queue, 4 MB bulk load) — the bottleneck moved

`perf record -F 999 --call-graph dwarf`, self-time:

- **base:** `__memp_alloc` **78.67%** self — the COOL-first sweep is the whole
  cost.
- **fix:** `__memp_alloc` **drops out of the top entirely**; the remaining cost
  is ordinary insert work — `__memp_fget` 21.9%, `__os_atomic_thread_fence`
  19.2%, `__memp_fput` 4.3%.

Raw: `results/r3-fix-2026-11/prof_base.txt`, `prof_fix.txt`.

## No-regression check: in-cache reads

`xrel_kv` workload C (reads), btree/hash, 16 GB cache, 1M records. `db_stat -m`
confirms **0 clean/dirty pages forced from the cache** in this workload — the
eviction sweep never runs, so the change is neutral by construction. Measured
t=16 cells agree (btree 1.06×, hash 0.95×, CV ≤ 6.4%). The t=32 cells are noisy
(CV 15–25%, bimodal read-contention on this 128-vCPU box) and are **not** a
function of the eviction change, which does not execute on an in-cache workload.
Raw: `results/r3-fix-2026-11/kv_ab.tsv`, `kv32.tsv`.
